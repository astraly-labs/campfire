# Code of Conduct

Campfire follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not accepted in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's [private reporting channel](https://github.com/astraly-labs/campfire/security/advisories/new). Maintainers will review reports confidentially and may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary.

This policy applies to the repository, issues, reviews, discussions, and other project-controlled community spaces. This text incorporates the Contributor Covenant 2.1, available under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
